Privacy policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

CanWas has no server. There is no account to create, no database behind it, and no analytics. Your boards are held by your own browser, and if you turn on sync they are copied to your own Google Drive. The author of CanWas never receives them, because there is nowhere for them to be sent.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is stored on the device you are using, by your browser, under the origin this page is served from.

IndexedDB canwas Your boards: the images you paste or drop, their positions and sizes, board names, and the text recognized inside each image. Also the OCR model weights once downloaded (about 31 MB).
canwas.preferredLang Your interface language, so it survives a reload.
canwas.drive.account Written only if you connect Google Drive: the name, email address and profile picture URL of the account you connected, so the app can offer to reconnect to the right one. It is not a credential and grants nothing.

CanWas sets no cookies and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, session recording or error-reporting service of any kind.

What leaves your device

Three third parties can receive a request from this app. In every case what they receive is the request itself — your IP address, the time, and your browser's user-agent string. None of them receives the contents of your boards, with the single exception of Google Drive, described below, which receives them because you asked it to.

Google Fonts — every page load of the app

The app loads its typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. This happens whether or not you sign in to anything. The page you are reading now loads no web font, so it makes no such request.

Hugging Face — the first time an image is read

Text recognition runs entirely on your device, but the model weights that do it are downloaded once, on demand, from huggingface.co, and then cached in your browser. Your images are never uploaded for recognition — no image ever leaves your device for this purpose.

Google Drive — only if you connect it

Sync is off until you press Connect. If you connect it, CanWas signs in through accounts.google.com and reads and writes files through www.googleapis.com.

  • The only scope requested is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which grants access to files this app itself creates and nothing else. CanWas cannot see, read or modify any other file in your Drive.
  • What is uploaded: your board records, your images re-encoded as WebP, and the recognized text for those images. They are stored in your Drive, in a folder you can open, and are subject to Google's terms and your own Drive settings.
  • The access token is never written to disk. It is held in memory for the life of the tab and expires in about an hour. What is stored is the account label described above, so the app knows it is worth asking Google for a token silently instead of showing you a Connect button you have already used.
  • Sharing a board with another person is not a feature of CanWas. Files it writes are private to your Drive account unless you share them yourself.

GitHub Pages — hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives a request for each page load and may log it; see GitHub's privacy statement. The author has no access to those logs beyond aggregate traffic counts.

Google API Services User Data Policy

CanWas's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google Drive is used only to provide the sync feature you turned on, is never transferred to anyone else, is never used for advertising, and is never read by a human.

Deleting your data

  • A board: delete it from the board menu inside the app. A deleted board is marked as deleted so that the deletion travels to your other devices, and its contents are erased after 30 days.
  • The model weights: the About panel in the app reports what is on disk and has a button to remove them.
  • Everything on this device: clear site data for this origin in your browser's settings. This removes all boards, images and preferences. It cannot be undone.
  • Drive access: revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking stops future syncing; it does not delete files already written, which you remove from Drive yourself.

Children

CanWas is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, because it collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the change is visible in the file's history.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to GitHub Issues, which is the project's only support channel. Note that issues are public — do not post anything private in one.

隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 8 月 20 日

CanWas 沒有伺服器，不用註冊帳號，背後沒有資料庫，也沒有任何分析工具。 白板存在你自己的瀏覽器裡；如果你開啟同步，會複製一份到你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟。CanWas 的作者永遠拿不到這些資料，因為根本沒有地方能把它們送出去。

存了什麼、存在哪裡

以下這些都存在你正在用的這台裝置上，由瀏覽器保管。

IndexedDB canwas 你的白板：貼上或拖進來的圖片、它們的位置與大小、白板名稱， 還有圖片裡辨識出來的文字。OCR 模型權重下載後（約 31 MB）也存在這裡。
canwas.preferredLang 介面語言，重新整理後不會跑掉。
canwas.drive.account 只有連結 Google 雲端硬碟才會寫入：帳號的名稱、電子郵件與大頭貼網址， 讓應用程式知道下次要重新連哪一個帳號。這不是憑證，也不授予任何權限。

CanWas 不使用 Cookie，也沒有分析工具、廣告、追蹤像素、 操作錄影，或任何錯誤回報服務。

什麼會離開你的裝置

有三個第三方可能收到這個應用程式發出的請求。它們拿到的都只是請求本身： 你的 IP 位址、時間，還有瀏覽器的 user-agent 字串。除了 Google 雲端硬碟以外（那是你自己開啟的），沒有任何一方會收到你白板的內容。

Google Fonts：每次打開應用程式都會發生

應用程式會從 fonts.googleapis.com 和 fonts.gstatic.com 載入字型，不管你有沒有登入都一樣。 你現在看的這一頁不用網路字型，所以不會發出這個請求。

Hugging Face：第一次辨識圖片的時候

文字辨識完全在你的裝置上跑，但要用的模型權重得先下載一次，來源是 huggingface.co，下載完就快取在瀏覽器裡。 你的圖片永遠不會為了辨識而被上傳，不會有任何一張圖片因為這樣離開你的裝置。

Google 雲端硬碟：只有你自己連結才會用到

  • 只會要求一個權限範圍： https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file， 只能存取這個應用程式自己建立的檔案。CanWas 看不到、讀不到，也改不了你雲端硬碟裡的其他檔案。
  • 會上傳的東西：你的白板紀錄、重新編碼成 WebP 的圖片， 還有這些圖片辨識出來的文字。它們存在你自己的雲端硬碟裡、一個你看得到的資料夾中。
  • 存取權杖永遠不會寫進磁碟，只留在分頁的記憶體裡， 大約一小時後過期。
  • 跟別人共用白板不是 CanWas 的功能。

GitHub Pages：網站代管

這個網站由 GitHub Pages 代管，每次載入頁面 GitHub 都會收到請求並可能記錄下來。 作者除了彙總的流量數字之外，拿不到這些記錄。

Google API 服務使用者資料政策

CanWas 使用與傳輸來自 Google API 的資訊時，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 規範。透過 Google 雲端硬碟取得的資料， 只用來提供你自己開啟的同步功能，不會轉交給任何其他人，不會用於廣告，也不會由人工讀取。

刪除你的資料

  • 單一白板：在應用程式的白板選單裡刪除。刪除的白板 會先標記成已刪除，好讓這個動作同步到你的其他裝置，內容則在 30 天後清除。
  • 模型權重：應用程式的「關於」面板有移除按鈕。
  • 此裝置上的全部資料：到瀏覽器設定裡清除本網站的資料。 此動作無法復原。
  • 雲端硬碟授權：到 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤銷。撤銷只會擋掉之後的同步，不會刪掉已經寫入的檔案，那些要你自己去雲端硬碟刪。

兒童

CanWas 不是為兒童設計的，也不會蒐集任何人、任何年齡的個人資訊，因為它本來就不蒐集任何個人資訊。

政策異動

這份政策如果有變動，上方的日期會跟著更新，改了什麼也都能在 檔案的變更紀錄裡看到。

聯絡方式
